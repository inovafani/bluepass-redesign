Questions

The answers you need.

Why wouldn't an AI just message the operators on WhatsApp?

A chat isn't a booking. To book, an AI needs live dates and prices, a way to take payment, and someone accountable if the trip falls through. A crew's WhatsApp has none of that, and since 15 January 2026 WhatsApp's business terms bar general-purpose AI assistants such as ChatGPT from its business platform. The AI companies don't onboard thousands of small crews one by one either: Google's hotel booking runs through Booking and Expedia, and Viator plugs into Gemini. Every category gets a supply partner. Bluepass is that partner for the ocean, and it replaces the booking site rather than adding a layer on top.

Isn't Bluepass just another middleman?

We replace the booking site rather than adding a layer on top: $10 in every $100 instead of $20 to $30. Every operator we surveyed prefers it: they keep more, more goes to the ocean, and they get content, marketing assets and creators sending guests direct. And the old model is under pressure: Spain fined Booking.com €413M in 2024, Caribbean hotels are fighting its commission on government taxes (May 2026), Google's AI now books hotels itself, and OpenAI bought an AI travel agent in November 2026.

What stops an operator going around Bluepass?

Their booking page, calendar and bookings run on Bluepass, and the guests come from our creators and our assistant. Going around us means going back to answering WhatsApp one guest at a time, and losing the guests we send.

What have you built that's hard to copy?

A booking system crews run from WhatsApp: a page built from each crew's own trips, one-tap accept, their calendar, and sync with Rezdy and FareHarbor. It's live in Australia today. The harder part to copy is the supply: every boat in a harbour, signed through creators they trust.

What is live today?

The booking assistant on the web and WhatsApp, booking in chat for connected Australian operators, creator links with their own portal, prebuilt operator pages with a one-click claim, and the booking calendar.
